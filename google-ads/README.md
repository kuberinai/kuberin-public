# Google Ads campaign assets for Kuber

These are starter copy assets based on the content currently shown on
[kubers.ai](https://kubers.ai/). The CSV is a copy/paste worksheet, not a
Google Ads Editor import file. Select the assets for the campaign you build;
you do not need to use every line.

## Files

- `assets.csv` — Search and Performance Max text assets, with character limits
  and suggested destination URLs.
- `videos/` — Silent 10-second H.264 MP4 concepts in vertical and landscape
  formats, for both the yantra and turtle creatives.
- `yantra-emblem.svg` — Kuber's gold yantra emblem from kubers.ai.
- `turtle-gold.svg` — Kuber's gold turtle mark from the site favicon.
- `make-video.swift` — Source used to render both videos with the yantra emblem
  and the four Ms, plus a walking turtle set with a Kuber wordmark.

Regenerate all four MP4s from the repository root with:

```sh
swift google-ads/make-video.swift
```

## Before launch

- Confirm the target countries, campaign objective, and landing pages.
- Check current Google Ads asset requirements and financial-services policies
  for every target country. Certification or other restrictions may apply.
- Review every asset and destination page for accuracy, required disclosures,
  and consistency with your offer. The copy makes no promise of returns.
- The videos keep the gold yantra emblem fixed at center while the four M labels
  orbit around it at a measured pace. Kuber branding and the website CTA are
  included. Confirm the final assets match your current brand guidelines before
  publishing.
- The alternate turtle videos show the Kuber wordmark above a walking gold
  turtle, with the four M labels orbiting around it. The labels complete an
  orbit in about 12 seconds.
- The videos are silent concept assets; add approved music or voiceover only
  after reviewing licensing and platform requirements.
- Add tracking parameters and conversion measurement before publishing.

## Suggested starting point

For a first test, use the Search headlines and descriptions with the homepage
as the landing page. Use the sitelinks to direct visitors to individual tools.
Performance Max assets are included as an optional starting set; supply
approved image and logo assets in Google Ads before publishing that campaign.
Use the video files as creative concepts and check current Google Ads
specifications and financial-services policies for each target country.
